Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026
No data linked to your identity is collected by the developer.

Caption Cleaner Pro ("the App," "we," "our") are built with your privacy as a core design principle. This policy explains what data the App interacts with and how it is handled.

Data we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs of any kind. We do not know how you use the App, how often you open it, or what content you process with it. Any usage metric shown in the app local to your device and iCloud.

Third-party services

The developer does not collect any data. If you choose to use the optional subtitle search and download feature, your device connects directly to OpenSubtitles, and the information it receives is handled under its own privacy policy. See "Subtitle search and download (OpenSubtitles)" below for details.

iCloud sync

Your video entries, caption tracks, filter lists, and app settings are stored in your personal, private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is encrypted and stored under your own Apple ID, is not accessible to us, and syncs only between your own devices, governed by Apple's iCloud privacy practices.

On-device processing

The App performs transcription, subtitle parsing, and optical character recognition entirely on your device using on-device machine learning and Apple's Vision framework. Audio, video, and caption content are never uploaded to any server we control.

Local network access

Caption Companion uses your local Wi-Fi network to discover and communicate with smart TVs for the purpose of muting or adjusting volume in sync with caption content. This communication happens only between your device and your TV, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and requires local network permission, which iOS will prompt you to grant.

Subtitle search and download (OpenSubtitles)

Caption Cleaner Pro can search for and download subtitles from OpenSubtitles.com, a third-party service. This feature is optional.

When you use it, your device connects directly to OpenSubtitles. Caption Cleaner Pro has no servers and does not receive, store, or relay this information. The requests may include:

  • Search terms, such as a movie or show title, episode number, or season number.
  • Your OpenSubtitles login credentials or access token, if you sign in with your own account.
  • Your IP address, which OpenSubtitles receives as part of any internet request.

Your OpenSubtitles account is separate from Caption Cleaner Pro. We never see your password, and your sign-in details are stored only on your device in the iOS Keychain. OpenSubtitles handles any information it receives under its own policies, which you can read at forum.opensubtitles.com/t/privacy-policy/6. We don't control and aren't responsible for their practices.

Subtitles you download are saved on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere by Caption Cleaner Pro.

Support communications

If you choose to contact us by email for support, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and do not share it with third parties.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. We have no advertising partners, analytics providers, or data brokers integrated into the App.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify users within the App.
