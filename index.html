Three things it's really good at

Caption filtering

Set the words and phrases you'd rather not hear, and Caption Cleaner Pro finds every variation across your MKV, MP4, and MOV files.

TV sync

Works with Samsung, LG, Sony, Vizio, Android TV, and Roku to mute and unmute right on cue, over your home network.

Private by design

This is a stand-alone app that runs on your phone. We don’t run any servers, so there’s nothing for us to collect.

How a movie night actually goes

1

Pick your file

Open a video or connect to your streaming session, and Caption Cleaner Pro reads the caption track.

2

It quietly filters

Lines matching your filter list get flagged, and your TV mutes for just that line.

3

You keep watching

Sound comes right back afterward. No pausing, no rewinding, no fumbling for the remote.

Built by one developer who wanted this to exist

I built Caption Cleaner Pro because I wanted to watch things with my family without the moments that make everyone reach for the remote at the same time. It started as a weekend project and turned into something I use almost every night.

It's an independent app — no company, no ad network, no data collection. Just something I built because I needed it, and I hope you find it useful too.
